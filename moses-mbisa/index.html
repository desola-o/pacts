Moses
Mbisa

Research Finance Administrator

Moses Mbisa, a Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants (ZICA) Licensed Accountant, brings a wealth of experience in strategic financial management, grant administration, statutory compliance, and process optimization to the PACTS team as a Research Finance Administrator.

With over five years of experience in finance, accounting, business administration, and tax technology, Moses is committed to leveraging technology and data analytics to enhance efficiency and streamline financial and administrative processes.

Moses possesses strong organizational and analytical skills, enabling him to adeptly manage diverse tasks within collaborative, multicultural teams.

Moses holds a Master of Science in Business Administration with a specialization in Financial Management and Accounting from Université Sidi Mohammed Ben Abdellah, Morocco. He is a Licentiate Member of the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants (ZICA), a Final Level (Advisory Professional) Chartered Accountant Zambia (CA Zambia) Student, and a holder of the PriceWaterhouse (PwC) Digital Acumen Badge.
